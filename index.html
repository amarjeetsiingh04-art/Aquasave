NATURE • FRESHWATER
THE BASICS

What is water conservation?

Water conservation means using water carefully, avoiding unnecessary wastage and protecting natural water resources. It includes small everyday habits as well as larger community efforts.

Freshwater is essential for drinking, farming, sanitation, industries and healthy ecosystems. Although Earth has plenty of water, only a small proportion is readily available freshwater.

Why is it important?

  • Helps reduce water shortages.
  • Protects rivers, lakes and groundwater.
  • Supports agriculture and food production.
  • Protects aquatic plants and animals.
  • Helps communities become more sustainable.
UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM

Common causes of
water wastage.

01

Leaking Taps

Small leaks can waste water continuously. Repair dripping taps and damaged pipes.

02

Long Showers

Using more water than necessary during bathing increases household consumption.

03

Inefficient Irrigation

Unplanned irrigation and watering during hot hours can lead to unnecessary water loss.

04

Industrial Waste

Poor water management and untreated wastewater can damage freshwater resources.

EVERYDAY ACTIONS

Smart Water-
Saving Tips

Simple habits. Meaningful results.

01 / AT HOME

Save water
where you live.

Turn off taps while brushing your teeth.
Repair leaking taps and pipes.
Take shorter showers.
Use a bucket instead of a running hose when practical.
Run dishwashers only with full loads.
Wash clothes with full laundry loads.
Keep drinking water in the refrigerator.
Avoid leaving water running while washing dishes.
Reuse suitable leftover water for plants.
Check your home regularly for hidden leaks.
02 / IN THE GARDEN

Grow green.
Use less water.

Water plants early in the morning.
Choose plants suited to your local climate.
Add mulch to help soil retain moisture.
Collect rainwater where permitted and safe.
Use drip irrigation where suitable.
Avoid watering during windy weather.
Group plants with similar watering needs.
Sweep outdoor areas instead of hosing them down.
03 / COMMUNITY

Make conservation
a shared effort.

Spread awareness about water conservation.
Report leaking school taps.
Keep nearby ponds and water bodies clean.
Participate in local tree-planting activities.
Organize water-awareness campaigns.
Encourage responsible water use in your community.

PROTECT OUR NATURAL RESOURCES
TAKE ACTION

How can we protect water?

Protecting water requires action at every level. Individuals can reduce wastage, communities can protect local water bodies, farmers can adopt efficient irrigation and industries can treat and reuse wastewater.

Governments and organizations also play an important role in protecting water resources for future generations.
